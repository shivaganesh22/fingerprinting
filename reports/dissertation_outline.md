# MSc Dissertation Chapter Outline (`reports/dissertation_outline.md`)

This document provides a chapter-by-chapter structural template for an MSc dissertation reporting on the browser fingerprinting measurement and countermeasure pipeline, mapped directly to the code artifacts, figures, and tables generated by `fpstudy`.

---

## Chapter 1: Introduction
- **1.1 Problem Statement:** The erosion of web privacy through stateless, cookieless tracking mechanisms.
- **1.2 Motivation:** Inadequacy of conventional cookie consent dialogs and persistent-identifier regulations against device fingerprinting under UK GDPR / ePrivacy Directive.
- **1.3 Research Questions:**
  - *RQ1:* What is the empirical prevalence of stateless browser fingerprinting (canvas, WebGL, AudioContext, font probing) across popular web domains?
  - *RQ2:* What proportion of fingerprinting scripts originate from third-party tracker domains versus first-party hosts?
  - *RQ3:* To what extent do consumer privacy countermeasures (network blocklists, privacy extensions, simulated noise) mitigate fingerprinting exposure, and do they introduce page breakage?
- **1.4 Contributions:** End-to-end reproducible measurement pipeline, paired within-domain countermeasure evaluation, and open-source verification framework.
- **1.5 Dissertation Structure:** Outline of subsequent chapters.

---

## Chapter 2: Literature Review
*(Refer to `reports/literature_review_leads.md` for candidate papers to verify)*
- **2.1 Evolution of Web Tracking:** From first-party HTTP cookies to third-party cookies, supercookies (ETags, HSTS), and stateless device fingerprinting.
- **2.2 Fingerprinting Vectors & Mechanisms:**
  - Canvas 2D rasterization entropy (Mowery & Shacham 2012).
  - WebGL hardware unmasking and shader precision (Acar et al. 2014).
  - AudioContext synthesizer discrepancies (Englehardt & Narayanan 2016).
  - Installed font probing via CSS metric comparisons (Fifield & Egelman 2015).
  - High-entropy navigator and screen characteristics.
- **2.3 Measurement Frameworks:** OpenWPM, FPDetective, FP-Inspector.
- **2.4 Privacy Countermeasures:** Tracker blocklists (EasyPrivacy, Disconnect), extension-based defenses (uBlock Origin, Canvas Defender), browser-native protections (Brave Shields, Firefox RFP, Tor Browser).
- **2.5 Gaps in Current Literature:** Need for paired, head-to-head empirical evaluations comparing blocklist efficacy vs noise injection while systematically tracking page breakage.

## Chapter 3: Measurement & Detection Methodology
*(Refer to `docs/methodology.md` and `docs/design.md`)*
- **3.1 Sampling Strategy:** Tranco top 1,000 domains, deduplication, URL candidate prioritization (`https://`, `https://www.`, `http://`).
- **3.2 Instrumentation Architecture:** Playwright init script injection (`hooks.js`), event batching, and stack trace parsing.
- **3.3 Third-Party Attribution:** Public Suffix List parsing via `tldextract` for eTLD+1 boundary detection.
- **3.4 Rule-Based Detection Classifiers:** Formally state criteria for Canvas, WebGL, Audio, Fonts, and Supporting Signals.
- **3.5 Experimental Countermeasure Conditions:** Baseline, EasyPrivacy/Disconnect blocklists, uBlock Origin, Canvas Defender simulation.
- **3.6 Statistical Framework:** Wilson 95% confidence intervals, paired McNemar's test (odds ratios and absolute risk reduction), Wilcoxon signed-rank test (Hodges-Lehmann estimator), and Holm-Bonferroni correction.
- **3.7 Denominator & Failure Accounting:** Successful visit cohort ($N_{\text{ok}}$) and failure taxonomy.

## Chapter 4: System Architecture & Implementation
*(Refer to `docs/design.md`)*
- **4.1 Pipeline Design:** Separation of crawling, storage, classification, and statistical reporting.
- **4.2 Crawler Engineering:** Concurrency semaphore, token-bucket per-domain rate limiting, and robots.txt parsing/caching.
- **4.3 Storage Engine:** SQLite WAL mode schema, transactional integrity, and JSONL archival format.
- **4.4 Condition Plugin Registry:** Extensible architecture for evaluating future privacy defenses.
- **4.5 Verification & Canaries:** Deterministic self-checks proving countermeasure activity before crawling.
